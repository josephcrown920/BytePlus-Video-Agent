# Josh - Street Lights (Music Video)

🎬 **Project**: Cinematic hip-hop music video featuring Josh
🎵 **Genre**: Rap / Hip-hop
🌃 **Setting**: Rainy night city streets with police lights
🎭 **Main Character**: Josh (rapper with red dreadlocks)

## 📋 Overview

A cinematic rap music video featuring Josh performing in a dramatic rain-soaked city street with police car lights creating a moody, atmospheric backdrop. The video combines defiance, confidence, and cinematic visual storytelling.

## 🎨 Visual Style

- **Color Palette**: Deep blues, neon reds, wet asphalt reflections, police siren lighting
- **Lighting**: Dramatic chiaroscuro, red/blue police flash, neon reflections, moody streetlights
- **Lens**: 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, cinematic bokeh
- **Aesthetic**: Gritty urban hip-hop, street photography, high contrast
- **Weather**: Heavy rain, wet reflective streets

## 🎭 Character: Josh

- Young Black male rapper with long **red/burgundy dreadlocks**
- Athletic build, goatee, confident swagger
- **Outfit 1**: Black BAPE shark t-shirt, khaki pants, white AF1s, multiple silver chains
- **Outfit 2**: Red/black NBA Josh jersey, rainbow sunglasses, cuban link chain
- **Signature prop**: Vintage silver microphone
